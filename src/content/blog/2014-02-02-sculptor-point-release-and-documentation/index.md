This post is just a couple quick updates on the [Sculptor Generator](http://sculptorgenerator.org) project.

Hot on the heels of the major [3.0 release](http://sculptorgenerator.org/2013/11/29/sculptor-300---a-major-overhaul/), [release 3.0.1](http://sculptorgenerator.org/2014/01/18/sculptor-301-released/) is out with additional improvements and examples. Kudos to Torsten, who's been on fire cranking out code and documentation.

I made my own small contributions to the documentation, with a [blog post](http://sculptorgenerator.org/2014/02/01/sculptor-shipping-example-and-overriding-templates/) on the shipping example project, which shows how to override Sculptor templates in your own project, and documentation on the Sculptor [overrides and extension mechanism](http://sculptorgenerator.org/documentation/advanced-tutorial#overrides-and-extension-mechanism).
